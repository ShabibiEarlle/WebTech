HTML Text Formatting

HTML stands for HyperText Markup Language.

Tag Explanations

<b> - Bold

The b tag makes text bold.

<strong> - Strong Importance

The strong tag shows text that is very important.

<i> - Italic

The i tag makes text italic.

<em> - Emphasis

The em tag emphasizes text.

<u> - Underline

The u tag underlines text.

<mark> - Highlight

The mark tag highlights text.

<small> - Small Text

The small tag makes text smaller.

<del> - Deleted Text

The del tag shows removed text: The meeting is on Monday.

<ins> - Inserted Text

The ins tag shows added text: The meeting is on Friday.

<sub> - Subscript

The sub tag lowers text: CO2

<sup> - Superscript

The sup tag raises text: 52
